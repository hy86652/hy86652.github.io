Led a team of seven analyzing a private-equity portfolio. We combined statistical testing (chi-square, logistic regression) with time-series regression on IRR and GCoC to evaluate how accurate deal projections were. The headline result was counterintuitive: there was no statistically significant link between EBITDA/Sales projection accuracy and exit success. We delivered an MVP dashboard and documentation for internal handoff.
