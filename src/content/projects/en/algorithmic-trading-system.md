A personal project: an algorithmic trading system that I prototype and iterate on with Claude Code.

The AI helps me move fast on strategy logic, from analyzing market data and looking for signal patterns to stress-testing assumptions. The rule I follow is that nothing is committed until I have checked it against real market behavior. Financial data is full of edge cases, and that is exactly where AI-generated code tends to be confidently wrong, so I override it when needed.
