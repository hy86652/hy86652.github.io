A full-stack platform where university students can list rooms, find them on a map, and swap housing. It was a response to campus housing shortages.

I designed the front-end logic as modular pieces and used the Leaflet and Mapbox APIs for map-based search.

The project won **2nd place at the UC Davis Big Bang! Business Competition (2025)**. The judges recognized both its technical feasibility and its market potential.

**Stack:** React, Leaflet, Mapbox
