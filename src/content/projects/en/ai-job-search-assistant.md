A course project (Cornell INFO 5125) where seven of us built an AI-powered job-search platform for international students.

The Streamlit prototype has three parts:

- a **visa-transparency dashboard** that updates every two weeks and flags conflicting data,
- **semantic resume-to-job matching** with FAISS and BERT, at about 85% matching accuracy,
- a **RAG Q&A assistant** built on ChromaDB.

My role was project management: the team contract, the work breakdown structure and Gantt chart that kept several parallel tracks in sync, the risk assessment, weekly tracking, and the final report.

**Stack:** Streamlit, FAISS, BERT, ChromaDB
