A Cornell capstone engagement with The Riverside Company, where I led a team of seven.

The question was simple to state: when a private-equity deal's EBITDA and sales projections turn out to be accurate, does the deal exit more successfully? We combined chi-square tests and logistic regression with time-series regression on IRR and GCoC, and looked for sector-level patterns and early signs of out- or under-performance.

## What we found

There was **no statistically significant relationship** between projection accuracy and exit success. This was counterintuitive, and it changed how the client thought about some of its underwriting assumptions.

## What I learned

- Leading seven people is mostly about keeping the question sharp. The analysis was the easy part.
- We changed our approach halfway through after client feedback. Doing that early saved the project.
- A null result is still a result, as long as you can show it is not just noise.

**Stack:** Python, logistic regression, time-series regression, chi-square tests, dashboard
