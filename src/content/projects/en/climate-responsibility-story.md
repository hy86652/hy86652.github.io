A data story that asks who is responsible for climate change, and shows that the answer depends on how you measure it.

It compares seven major emitters on four metrics that are all valid and that all disagree: total annual emissions, per-capita emissions, cumulative historical emissions, and consumption-based emissions. The same data gives four different verdicts depending on which idea of fairness you apply.

I cleaned and reshaped the Our World in Data country-year panel, with records going back to 1750, and built the story with Closeread. A sticky visualization panel updates as you scroll.

**Stack:** R, Quarto, Closeread, Our World in Data
