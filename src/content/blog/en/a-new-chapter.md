This site used to be a job-search portfolio. It had a big hero banner, a row of impressive-looking numbers, and project cards that animated as you scrolled. It did its job. Now that the search is over, that job is done.

So I am giving it a new one. From now on this is a **notebook** for my career, a place to keep track of:

- **what I work on**: projects, decisions, and how they turned out;
- **what I learn**: new tools, techniques, and the mistakes that taught me something;
- **what I think**: the occasional longer reflection on work and craft.

## How it is organized

- **Blog** holds longer posts like this one: retrospectives and things worth thinking through.
- **Notes** holds short entries, often just a few paragraphs about something I learned that day. Writing small is the only way I will keep writing.
- **Work** and **Projects** are the stable record of where I have been and what I have built.

Everything is written in both English and Chinese. The switch is in the top-right corner.

## Why bother

Memory is a bad archive. A year from now I will not remember why I chose one approach over another, or what that confusing bug actually was. Writing it down costs a few minutes today and saves hours later. And if any of it helps someone else, even better.
